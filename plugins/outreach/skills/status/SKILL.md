# Outreach status

Call `get_status` and give the person a short summary:

- anything still to set up (`setup`), in the order it has to happen;
- drafts waiting for their approval, and that they approve them on the website;
- first emails sent, replies, bounces, and follow-ups due;
- who replied, with the reminder that replies are answered in Gmail, and that a no is still worth a reply asking who else they should talk to;
- whether sending is paused (`sendingEnabled` false), which only the admin can change.

Suggest the one most useful next step. Keep it to a few lines.
